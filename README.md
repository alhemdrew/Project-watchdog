# Project Watchdog

### Nigeria-first community security & incident intelligence

Watchdog is a Nigeria-first community security and incident intelligence platform designed to help people understand what is happening around them through structured reporting, evidence, corroboration, verification, and geographic context.

Watchdog is not a generic social network and it is not a truth detector. Reports, evidence, corroboration, and verification are intentionally separated so that people can tell the difference between a claim, supporting material, and a verified status.

## What Watchdog does

- Local signal discovery and geographic context for community safety concerns
- Structured incident reporting with supporting context and location details
- Evidence capture and media-backed submissions
- Search and discovery across relevant reports and activity
- Corroboration and trust-aware review workflows
- Verification and moderation processes for signal quality
- Community discussion and alerting around local events

## Trust model

Watchdog treats trust as a process, not a popularity score.

REPORTED → REVIEWING → CORROBORATED → VERIFIED → ACTIVE → RESOLVED

This distinction matters:

- a report is not automatically a fact
- evidence is supporting context, not automatic truth
- corroboration increases confidence
- verification is an explicit review outcome
- human moderation remains important

## Product areas

### Discovery

Watchdog helps people understand what is happening in a neighborhood, region, or wider area without collapsing local context into a single vague signal.

### Incident reporting

Users can provide structured reports with geographic context and supporting material so that a report can be reviewed in context.

### Map and geographic intelligence

The platform incorporates geographic awareness, region-based context, and location-scoped discovery.

### Evidence and corroboration

Evidence, related reports, and corroboration are treated as separate layers from the original claim, which helps maintain clarity during fast-moving local events.

### Verification and moderation

The platform distinguishes between reported information, corroborated context, verified status, and moderator review.

### Search and discovery

Users can search and filter relevant incident activity in a way that supports situational awareness rather than social noise.

### Community and alerts

Community discussions, notifications, and local context remain part of the product experience without pretending that discussion alone is verified fact.

## Current status

This repository is a public product showcase for the current Watchdog direction. It presents the product concept, product surfaces, and technical foundation without including the private implementation or active development codebase.

The project currently includes the implemented product surfaces described above, while future intelligence and broader automation layers remain planned work.

## Roadmap

Planned work includes:

- stronger regional intelligence and contextual summary layers
- richer corroboration and review workflows
- more robust official-source and organization integration
- improved alerting and situational briefing capabilities
- broader public-safety and local intelligence coverage
